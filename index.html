Capabilities

What CORAL does

Regulatory Test Mapping

Map executable tests directly to legal clauses and audit controls using a structured reference taxonomy — making compliance provable, not just claimable.

Law & Standard Manifests

Machine-readable YAML manifests for each framework, with clause summaries in English and French and links to authoritative official sources.

CI/CD Integration

Run CORAL in every build. Catch regulatory and audit violations continuously — not during an external audit or regulatory investigation.

Clause-Level Reporting

Every finding maps to a specific clause or control, with remediation guidance developers can act on without requiring legal expertise.

License Scanning

Surface open source license metadata and policy conflicts across your dependency tree — included alongside regulatory compliance auditing.

Bilingual by Design

All manifests and reports support English and French. Built for Canadian regulatory environments and ready for international teams.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is CORAL free to use?

Yes — for individuals, non-profits, academic institutions, and companies under $1M ARR, CORAL is completely free. Commercial users above that threshold are asked to contribute back through code or sponsorship proportional to their scale.

Is CORAL open source?

CORAL is source-available under the CORAL Community License (CCL) v1.0. It is not OSI-certified open source, but the full source code is publicly available on GitHub and free to use for most users.

What compliance frameworks are currently supported?

CORAL currently supports GDPR (EU) and Law 25 (Québec). PIPEDA, HIPAA, CCPA, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 are coming soon. See the full roadmap on GitHub.

How is CORAL different from a traditional compliance audit tool?

Traditional audit tools assess documentation and process. CORAL asserts compliance at the code level — your tests either pass or fail against specific legal clauses and audit controls, giving you a continuous, developer-native compliance signal rather than a point-in-time assessment.

How do I report a security vulnerability?

Please do not open a public GitHub issue. Send a private report to security@coral-oss.dev. We acknowledge within 48 hours and aim to resolve confirmed vulnerabilities within 30 days.
