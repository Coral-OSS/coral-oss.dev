Preamble

The CORAL Community License is designed to ensure that the CORAL project remains free and accessible to individuals, researchers, and small organizations, while requiring commercial entities that derive economic benefit from its use to contribute proportionally to its continued development and maintenance.

This license is not an OSI-approved open source license. It is a source-available license that prioritizes long-term project sustainability.

Definitions

"Software"
The CORAL source code, documentation, and associated files distributed under this license.
"You"
The individual or legal entity exercising rights under this license.
"Commercial Use"
Any use of the Software in connection with a revenue-generating activity, including deploying the Software internally to support business operations, incorporating the Software into a commercial product or service, or using the Software to perform services for third parties in exchange for compensation.
"Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)"
The annualized value of all subscription, license, and service revenue of You and any affiliates, parent companies, or subsidiaries under common control.
"Meaningful Contribution"
A pull request reviewed and merged into the official CORAL repository by a maintainer. Contributions must represent a substantive improvement — a new feature, bug fix, security patch, significant documentation improvement, or test suite addition. Minor formatting changes, typo fixes, or trivial modifications do not qualify.
"Sponsorship"
A recurring financial contribution made through the official CORAL sponsorship channels as listed in the project's README.
"Grace Period"
90 days following the date on which You first meet a commercial tier threshold.

Tier 1 — Community (Free)

Applies to: Individuals using the Software for personal, educational, or non-commercial purposes; non-profit organizations; academic and government institutions; companies or organizations with less than USD $1,000,000 in ARR.

Rights granted: You may use, copy, modify, and distribute the Software and derivative works, for any purpose, subject to the conditions in the Conditions section.

Obligations: Attribution only.

Tier 2 — Commercial (Contribute)

Applies to: Companies or organizations with ARR between USD $1,000,000 and USD $50,000,000 engaging in Commercial Use.

Rights granted: You may use, copy, modify, and deploy the Software in connection with your commercial operations, subject to the conditions below.

Annual obligation — satisfy at least one:

  1. Make at least one Meaningful Contribution to the official CORAL repository; or
  2. Maintain an active Sponsorship of at least USD $500 per year through official project sponsorship channels.

Tier 3 — Enterprise (Sponsor)

Applies to: Companies or organizations with ARR exceeding USD $50,000,000 engaging in Commercial Use.

Rights granted: You may use, copy, modify, and deploy the Software in connection with your commercial operations, subject to the conditions below.

Annual obligation — satisfy at least one:

  1. Maintain an active Sponsorship of at least USD $5,000 per year through official project sponsorship channels; or
  2. Make at least three Meaningful Contributions per calendar year. Financial sponsorship may be substituted for contributions at maintainer discretion.
